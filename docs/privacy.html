Family Screen privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Family Screen turns an iPad into a shared screen for a family's two homes. It is made by an independent developer (contact: support@example.com). We do not run any server, show ads, or sell or track anything.

What the app stores and where

  • Photos, photo notes and messages you add are saved on your iPad. If you link two homes, they are also saved in your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit) and shared only with the people who joined your family through your invite link. We, the developer, cannot see them.
  • Only photos you choose are shared, from the app or from Share ▸ Family Screen on your iPhone. Each shared copy is made smaller (at most 2048 pixels) and re-saved without its location and other camera data (EXIF). A family keeps up to 300 photos; photos are removed after 90 days unless someone chooses to keep them.
  • iCloud Shared Album (optional): if you pick one in Settings, the app reads that album on this iPad to show it. It asks for Photos access for this, and does not upload or copy those photos anywhere.
  • Settings (home name, language, cities, the FaceTime contact you choose, news feed addresses) stay on your iPad.
  • Calendar events are read on the iPad to show them on screen, only if you allow it. They are never copied or sent anywhere.

Services the app talks to

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit to sync between your homes (see Apple's privacy policy).
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit) or Open-Meteo for weather. Only the two cities' map positions are sent, not who you are.
  • News feeds you add yourself. The app downloads the feed from that website to show headlines; tapping one opens the publisher's page.
  • Apple App Store handles the one-off Family Screen Plus purchase. We never see your payment details; the app only asks Apple whether this Apple ID has bought it.
  • FaceTime opens Apple's FaceTime app when you tap the call button.

Children

Photos of children are only shared inside your own invited family. The app has no public sharing, no profiles and no chat with strangers.

Content rules

Do not post anything abusive or illegal. Any home can hide another home's posts, report a photo or message to us from its menu, and the person who set up the family can remove a home at any time.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any photo or message from its menu (press and hold).
  • Settings ▸ Leave this family removes you from a family you joined.
  • Settings ▸ Delete family and all photos (for the person who set it up) deletes everything from iCloud for everyone.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on that iPad.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page. Questions: support@example.com.
